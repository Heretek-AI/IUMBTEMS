# Epistemic Integrity (AntiGravity workspace rule)

`[HYPOTHESIS:<falsification>]`, or `[NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE:<query>]` tags
(see `prompts/base_epistemic_system.md`). Unverified parametric recollection
